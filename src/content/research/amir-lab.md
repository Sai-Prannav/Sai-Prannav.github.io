The lab's building a 6-DOF soft parallel robot for transcatheter cardiac intervention, funded by the NIH. On the last iteration I led the redesign of the flexible joints. For the next revision, which uses a new catheter, I'm fabricating and integrating those joints.

I also do SolidWorks CAD for test fixtures, and I helped build the bench platform. I'm keeping design details private until publication.

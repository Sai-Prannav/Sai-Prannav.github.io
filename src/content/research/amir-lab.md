The lab is building a 6-DOF soft parallel robot for transcatheter cardiac intervention, funded by the NIH.

On the previous iteration I led the redesign of the flexible joints. That robot was built but couldn't be controlled well, because the catheters weren't accurate enough. The next iteration uses a different catheter, and I'm fabricating and integrating the flexible joints for that hardware revision.

I also do SolidWorks CAD for test fixtures and helped build the bench platform. I don't post design details here while the work is still being protected.

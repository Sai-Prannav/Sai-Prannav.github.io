The lab studies a carangiform fish robot driven by a bistable hair-clip mechanism.

I built the lab's 3D-printed thrust-testing rig, from the print-versus-buy analysis through fabrication and assembly. It was finished in September 2026. The rig is now getting load cells, HX711 amplifiers and an Arduino so we can measure thrust. That part is still in progress.

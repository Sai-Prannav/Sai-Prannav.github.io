Two papers from this lab got accepted to TFEC 2027. I helped develop and test the algorithms, and I collected the data.

The window paper estimates heat loss and gain through windows from infrared images. Its machine learning part is a proposed framework, so it doesn't report trained-model results. The other paper uses machine learning to estimate tumor blood perfusion during hyperthermia. I don't claim results for the window paper's model, because there aren't any yet.

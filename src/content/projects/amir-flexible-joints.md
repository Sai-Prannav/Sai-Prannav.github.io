## The problem

The last iteration got built, but its catheters weren't accurate enough to control it precisely. The next one uses a different catheter, so the hardware gets revised before the lab's next experiment.

## My part

Manufacturing. The joint design carries over from the last iteration, so I'm making the joints and connecting them to the new catheter assembly. I don't need to design anything new.

## What's shown here

Nothing from the device. The design's protected until publication, so there are no images, dimensions or materials on this page.

## Where it stands

My joints are in progress, and the lab's next experiment waits on the revised hardware.
